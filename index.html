Privacy policy for ARBSOLUTIONS Pin Manager

Last updated: 26 November 2026

ARBSOLUTIONS Pin Manager is a private tool run by ARBSOLUTIONS (the Netherlands) for its own Pinterest business account. It has no other users.

What we collect from Pinterest

When the owner connects the account through Pinterest's sign-in (OAuth), we receive an access token and a refresh token for that account. Through the Pinterest API we read the owner's boards and the analytics totals (impressions, saves, outbound clicks) of the Pins this tool published. We do not collect data of other Pinterest users.

How we use it

Only to publish Pins of our own products to our own boards, after the owner approved each Pin, and to show the owner which products interest people. We do not use Pinterest data for advertising to others, profiling, training AI models, or any other purpose.

Storage and protection

Tokens, Pin IDs, board IDs and analytics totals are stored on our own server in the European Union, readable only by the tool and its administrator. Secrets are kept out of source code. Connections to Pinterest use HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Pinterest data with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Analytics totals are kept as long as the Pin exists. If the account is disconnected, we delete the tokens. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings (Security, Apps). Ask us to delete stored data at infoarbsolutionszzp@gmail.com and we do so within 30 days.
